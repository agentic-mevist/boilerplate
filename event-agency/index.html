How it works

Four steps between hello and showtime.

  1. 01

    Listen

    Every event starts as a conversation: goals, guests, budget, appetite for the unexpected. We ask more questions than you expect.

  2. 02

    Design

    Concept, narrative, spatial design — and a budget that behaves. You approve a creative treatment before a single vendor is booked.

  3. 03

    Produce

    Venues, technical, catering, artists, permits — and contingency plans for the contingency plans. One producer, one point of contact.

  4. 04

    Show time

    On the day, we run everything and you carry nothing. Afterwards: a debrief, the numbers, and the photos worth framing.

The studio

Obsessive about the details you'll never see.

Solstice is eighteen producers, designers and technical directors based in Vienna, working wherever the brief takes us. We believe the best events feel inevitable — as if they couldn't have happened any other way.

That feeling is manufactured. It takes honest budgets, ruthless run-of-shows and a decade-deep black book of venues, artists and crews. We bring all three.

  • One senior producer per project, first call to final invoice
  • Transparent budgets — line by line, no padding
  • Sustainability written into every rider and load-out
  • A 24/7 show-week hotline, answered by a human
