How metaphoric cards work

Metaphoric (associative) cards are a projective tool used in counselling and self-exploration. The image on a card has no fixed meaning — whatever you see in it comes from you. The associations that surface can reveal feelings, memories and needs that are hard to reach by thinking alone.

  1. Hold a question in mind — or simply stay open.
  2. Draw a card from the deck and look at it without hurry.
  3. Notice what catches your eye first, the mood, where you would be in the picture.
  4. Name your association — click the card and write a few words.
  5. Arrange the cards on the table; their positions can speak too.

There are no right answers and no fortune-telling here. This is a prototype for reflection, not a substitute for therapy. Everything you write stays in your browser.
